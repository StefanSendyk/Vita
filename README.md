# Vita

Build the CV your dream university is looking for.

High-school students (14+) pick their top 5 universities and a course at each. Vita compares their profile with what each university typically expects, lists the gaps, and suggests small CV add-ons to complete in 2-week sprints.

## Features
- **Home hub:** university search, quick tiles, profile progress, stats
- **100 universities** with readiness %, a "How is this calculated?" breakdown, Dream/Target/Safety labels, typical deadlines and links to official requirements
- **Plan:** a roadmap by age, upcoming deadlines with countdowns, list-balance advice, and .ics calendar export for deadlines and sprint reminders
- **185 CV add-ons**, filtered by field, category and region (online/global, UK, US, EU, Poland, Germany, Canada, Australia)
- **Sprints & streaks:** ×1/×1.25/×1.5/×2 multipliers, streak bonus, streak freezes and 12 badges
- **LinkedIn-style CV:** photo, banner, grades (cards or a quick table editor), exams, courses, experience, skills, languages and profile strength. PDF export in 3 templates, plus a read-only share link
- **Personal statement helper:** UCAS 3-question format, Common App essay or motivation letter, with ideas pulled from your CV and live counters

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in a browser. There is no build step.

## Data disclaimer
Admissions figures are approximate. Always check the official university admissions pages.
